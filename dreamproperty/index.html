Property Management

We look after everything day to day, so your rental runs smoothly and you stay informed.

What's included

  • Rent collection and arrears monitoring
  • Tenant communication and lease administration
  • Routine property management and inspections
  • Financial reporting and water invoice processing
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Tribunal representation and insurance claim help
  • Healthy Homes compliance support

Tenant Find & Letting

We manage the whole process of finding a tenant, from pricing and marketing through to move-in.

What's included

  • Rental appraisal and pricing advice
  • Professional property marketing
  • Property viewings
  • Credit and background checks
  • Employment and reference checks
  • Tribunal history checks
  • Tenancy paperwork and lease preparation
  • Move-in inspection report

Healthy Homes Compliance

We help you meet New Zealand's Healthy Homes Standards, from checking your property through to arranging the work.

Areas we support

  • Healthy Homes assessments
  • Heating and heat pump coordination
  • Insulation requirements
  • Ventilation requirements
  • Moisture and drainage solutions
  • Compliance guidance and paperwork
  • Ongoing support and advice

Property Inspections

Regular checks help us catch problems early and keep your property in good condition.

Our approach

  • Routine property inspections
  • Detailed reports with photos
  • Early sign of leaks, dampness, damage and wear
  • Healthy Homes and safety checks
  • Maintenance recommendations
  • Clear owner updates and follow-up

Rent Collection

We keep a close eye on rent, so payments stay on track and issues get sorted quickly.

Our process

  • Daily rent monitoring
  • Fast tenant follow-up
  • Arrears notices and formal breach notices where needed
  • Help arranging rent payments through WINZ
  • Tenancy Tribunal applications where needed
  • Debt recovery referrals after a tenancy ends
  • Regular landlord updates

Maintenance & Repairs

Good maintenance means preventing problems, not just reacting to them.

What we coordinate

  • Tenant maintenance requests
  • Trusted Auckland contractors
  • Plumbing and electrical work
  • Appliance repairs and general building work
  • Urgent repairs and scheduled maintenance
  • Repair history and follow-up tracking
  • Preventative maintenance planning
  • Property improvement ideas
