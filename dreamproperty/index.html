About Dream Property

Dream Property is the repurposed identity of Auckland Property Rentals & Management, bringing the same experience and commitment into a more distinctive, modern brand.

Locally owned and operated, our approach combines professional property management, proactive care and clear communication to protect investments and support long-term rental performance.

02 / Letting

Tenant Find & Letting

We manage the letting journey from pricing and marketing through to screening, documentation and move-in.

What's included

  • Rental appraisal and market positioning
  • Professional property marketing
  • Property viewings
  • Credit and background checks
  • Employment and reference verification
  • Tribunal history checks
  • Tenancy documentation and lease preparation
  • Move-in inspection reporting
03 / Compliance

Healthy Homes Compliance

Support for landlords navigating New Zealand's Healthy Homes Standards, from assessment through to coordinated upgrades.

Areas we support

  • Healthy Homes assessments
  • Heating and heat pump coordination
  • Insulation requirements
  • Ventilation requirements
  • Moisture ingress and drainage solutions
  • Compliance guidance and documentation
  • Ongoing support and advice
04 / Oversight

Property Inspections

Regular oversight helps identify problems early, protect property condition and maintain a strong owner-tenant relationship.

Our approach

  • Routine property inspections
  • Detailed reports with photos
  • Early identification of leaks, dampness, damage and wear
  • Healthy Homes and safety checks
  • Maintenance recommendations
  • Clear owner communication and follow-up
05 / Income

Strict Rent Collection

A proactive process focused on protecting rental income and addressing arrears before they become bigger problems.

Our process

  • Daily rent monitoring
  • Immediate tenant follow-up
  • Arrears notifications and formal breach notices where required
  • MSD rent redirection assistance
  • Tenancy Tribunal applications where appropriate
  • Debt recovery referrals after tenancy
  • Regular landlord updates
06 / Care

Maintenance & Repairs

Smart maintenance is about preventing problems, not simply reacting to them.

What we coordinate

  • Tenant maintenance requests
  • Vetted Auckland contractors
  • Plumbing and electrical services
  • Appliance repairs and general building work
  • Urgent repairs and scheduled maintenance
  • Repair history and follow-up tracking
  • Preventative maintenance planning
  • Property improvement recommendations
